3.CLINICAL NARRATIVE-
A 24-year-old female was prescribed oral amoxicillin/clavulanic acid for a bacterial respiratory infection. She had noknown previous drug allergy. On the third day of treatment, she developed a generalized itchy raised rash consistentwith urticaria. No other new medicine or relevant exposure was identified during the same period.The suspected medicine was discontinued. An oral antihistamine was administered for symptomatic management.The rash began to resolve after withdrawal and completely resolved within approximately 48 hours. No recurrencewas observed during follow-up. Rechallenge was not attempted because re-exposure was not clinically necessary.The temporal relationship, improvement after withdrawal, lack of a more convincing alternative explanation, andknown potential for beta-lactam hypersensitivity support a drug-related reaction.
